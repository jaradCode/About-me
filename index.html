CHAPTER 01 / 09

My
Beginning.

WHERE IT STARTED

Port St Johns, Eastern Cape.

My name is Ntsikelelo, and I come from Port St Johns in the Eastern Cape. I'm writing this on Saturday, 16 August 2026, at 17:31. I suddenly had the idea to create this documentary for myself — a place to record where I come from, where I'm going, and what I want to become.

WHO I AM

Curious by nature.

I'm a curious person. I love reading, learning new things and moving differently from other people.

MY PERSONALITY

Still becoming.

I used to be quite disorganised and lost. I didn't always know what I wanted. Today, I believe I am different from who I was, and I want to continue becoming different.

WHERE I'M GOING

I want to build.

I am currently doing B.Com Accounting at the University of Fort Hare. Computer Science and coding only became a serious interest for me around June 2026. I want to continue my degree while learning technology on the side. Eventually, I want to own businesses and become a developer in the coding field.

CHAPTER 02 / 09

My
Story.

CHILDHOOD

Growing up.

Growing up in Port St Johns was not a bad experience for me. The environment, schools, clinic, shops and other things were there. My childhood was good.

FAMILY

We never had much.

My family never had much financially, but my parents made sure that my siblings and I had food, clothes, education and the things we needed.

HOME

My family matters.

I love my family a lot. They are the people I trust more than anyone and anything in this world.

WORK

My parents.

My mother used to clean at a local church and earned around R1,000 a month. My father worked as a security guard at the school where I was studying and earned around R1,500.

CHURCH

A big part of childhood.

My family loved church. We used to go almost every day, even when we did not feel like going. It became one of the things that shaped me.

LESSONS

Mistakes taught me.

I've made many mistakes. The most important thing for me is that I learned from them. I've also watched how certain choices affect people and that has helped me decide what kind of person I do not want to become. My past wasn't perfect, but it taught me a lot.

CHAPTER 03 / 09

Journey.

2022

Failing Grade 10.

I failed Grade 10. It became one of the moments that made me want to become better and take my future more seriously.

2022 — 2025

Learning to learn.

Over the next few years, I started paying more attention to books and knowledge. I developed a love for learning and seeking knowledge.

JUNE 2026

Discovering coding.

Around June 2026, I discovered how much I love Computer Science, coding and technology. This was when that path started becoming important to me.

2026

University.

I applied late and had to choose Accounting because it was the best course available to me at the time. I got into the University of Fort Hare and started B.Com Accounting.

NOW

Still moving.

I am doing well now. I have no regrets about where I am. I'm proud that I made it this far and I'm continuing to work towards something bigger.
