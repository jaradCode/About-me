01 — WHERE IT STARTED
This is where I begin.
My name is Ntsikelelo, and I come from Port St Johns in the Eastern Cape. I'm writing this on Saturday, 16 August 2026, at 17:31, at a moment when I suddenly had the idea to create this documentary for myself — a place to record where I come from, where I'm going, and what I want to become.
02 — WHO I AM
Curious by nature.
I'm a curious person who loves reading, learning new things, and moving differently from other people. I wouldn't call myself a genius; I'm a hard worker who developed a real love for learning and discovering things beyond what I already know.
03 — TODAY
Still becoming.
I'm currently a first-year B.Com Accounting student at the University of Fort Hare. I used to be quite disorganised and lost, but now I feel different. I want to keep learning, building, and becoming someone different.
04 — WHERE I'M GOING
I want to build.
Around June 2026, I discovered how much I enjoy Computer Science, coding, and technology. I want to continue with Accounting while learning those things on the side. Eventually, I want to own businesses and become a developer.

01 — HOME
Port St Johns.
Growing up in Port St Johns was not a bad experience for me. There are schools, clinics, shops, churches, and the basic things that make a community a home. I had a good childhood, and although my family never had much financially, my parents always made sure that my siblings and I had food, clothes, education, and the things we needed.
02 — FAMILY
We never had much.
My mother used to clean at a local church and earned around R1,000 a month. My father worked as a security guard at the school I attended and earned around R1,500. They worked in different years. Today, neither of them is working, and my family still depends on the SASSA grant.
03 — CHURCH
Part of my childhood.
My family loved church. We used to go almost every day, even when we didn't feel like going. Church was simply part of our lives, and growing up with it became one of the things that shaped me.
04 — LESSONS
Mistakes taught me.
I've made many mistakes, but I believe the most important thing is learning from them. I've also watched how certain choices can negatively affect people's lives, and those experiences have helped me decide what kind of person I don't want to become. My past wasn't perfect, but it taught me a lot.
